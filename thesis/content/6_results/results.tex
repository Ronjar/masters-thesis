In summary, the results draw a complex picture of the impact of settlement schemes on vegetation and forest cover.
Vegetation indices show a small but consistent negative impact on vegetational health, robust to different specifications and subsamples,
especially for conventional schemes, long periods of registration, and the exclusion of settlements with unknown boundaries.
In contrast, the spatial RDD results for deforestation and degradation indicate a more complex situation.
Significant effects are only found for deforestation, and only in the base regression and the Donut specification.
Results for degradation are not significant in any of the specifications, and robustness checks show that the results for deforestation are not robust to different bandwidths or subsamples.
The contrasting outcomes of the two dependent variables, the different influence of using the same subsample criteria,
and the overall small magnitude of treatment effects will be discussed in the next chapter.
